## 4. News Roundup

**REIZ rights offer results (16 Nov, LuSE SENS)** — Real Estate Investments Zambia published the outcome of its rights offer. The counter closed 8.33% lower on the USD-quoted line; the dilution is the driver, not property valuations.

**LuSE moves to T+1 settlement pilot (12 Nov, LuSE SENS)** — The exchange confirmed commencement of a pilot transition to a T+1 securities settlement cycle from the current T+2 convention. Faster settlement cuts counterparty exposure, frees collateral and is a prerequisite for deepening secondary-market liquidity. Brokers and custodians run the pilot before full cut-over.

**ZRA tightens mineral value-chain monitoring (14 Nov, Zambia 24)** — Commissioner General Dingani Banda told the ZACCI 2027 Budget Copperbelt Symposium that ZRA will strengthen value-chain monitoring after copper delivered more than **K13bn in mineral royalties in 2025**. Only K64m came from a single gold operator despite extensive deposits, and ZRA paid over K25bn in VAT refunds last year with fictitious-export claims a live problem. ZACCI president Anthony Kabaghe put outstanding VAT refunds owed to business at over K11.2bn.

**ZCCM-IH targets copper trading, energy and formalised gold (14 Nov)** — Board chairperson Phesto Musonda said ZCCM-IH is investing in energy to support the 10,000MW-by-2031 target and is working to formalise gold production. Separately reported this week, the company plans to enter copper trading directly to capture more revenue from mineral sales rather than holding passive stakes. ZCCM-IH closed unchanged at K148.
